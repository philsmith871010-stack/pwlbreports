    What the page found

    How the deal register works

    Three doors

    Download the template gives you the workbook: a Settings sheet, one sheet each for term investments, call accounts and money market funds, loans and pooled funds, and a Lists sheet holding the drop-downs. Point at your copy reads the workbook you have filled in, where it sits; nothing is uploaded and the file is never written. Try it with the examples reads the template as it comes.

    The workbook is the contract

    Each holding sheet is an Excel table with fixed headers; add rows beneath the last and the page finds them. The drop-downs are the vocabulary the page understands: a word off the list is flagged, free text is left alone. The as-at date on Settings is the date every balance is true at. HRA on Settings decides whether each loan says which fund it belongs to.

    Keep your history

    Do not delete a deal when it matures or a loan when it is repaid. A row dated on or before the as-at date is history: it is greyed in the replica, left out of the position, and read for the activity figures — what was placed and matured, raised and repaid, in the period you choose. For a loan repaid before the as-at date, Principal is the amount repaid. Money on call and in money market funds is a balance at the as-at date and never activity.

    What the figures mean

    A term deal counts at its nominal: the face value where one is given, the principal otherwise. A loan's principal is what is outstanding at the as-at date; EIP and annuity loans are run forward by their profile and backward for the instalments already paid. A LOBO is taken at its next call date. Maturity bands are counted in whole calendar months from the as-at date. Interest figures are indicative, on deal terms, actual days over 365: a figure to set against the ledger, never the ledger.

    In the report tool

    On the tool's What you hold step, Use the deal register fills the debt and investment rows and both maturity grids from the same workbook, and step 3's projections run off from the dates loan by loan. The counterparty step sets each limit against what the register holds. The draft can be summary, the tables as now, or detailed, with every chart from here. A monitoring report's activity section carries the period's placed, matured, raised and repaid. Download the dashboard writes this page with your figures and your policy in it, as one file to send on.

    Privacy

    The page keeps no record of your deals, stores nothing between visits, and makes no request of its own. The report tool keeps a snapshot of the rows it read inside your own report file, and nowhere else.
    Read-only, by design: this page keeps no record of your deals, stores nothing between visits, and is not a treasury management system. Your workbook is the record; the page is a reader for it. Browsers from 2023 on can inflate the workbook natively; an older one will say so.
