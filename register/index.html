Deal register

prototype · reads your workbook in the browser

Keep your deals in a spreadsheet you own. Point this page at it and it reads your investments and borrowing as they stand, draws the maturity ladders and what is still out at each year end, and shows you every row as it read it. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is written back, and the page makes no request of its own.

Download the template

An Excel workbook with a Settings sheet and one sheet each for term investments, call accounts and MMFs, loans and pooled funds, with example rows to replace with your own. The drop-downs are the vocabulary the page understands.

    What the page found

    Read-only, by design: this page keeps no record of your deals, stores nothing between visits, and is not a treasury management system. Your workbook is the record; the page is a reader for it. Browsers from 2023 on can inflate the workbook natively; an older one will say so.
